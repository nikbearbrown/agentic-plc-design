SYNTHETIC SPECIFICATION EXCERPT (invented for training; not from any real project)

SECTION 40 95 13 - PUMP STATION CONTROL PANELS

1.1 SUBMITTALS
The Contractor shall submit shop drawings, a bill of materials and a written control narrative for review before fabrication.
Shop drawings shall be prepared in accordance with UL 508A and shall bear the UL 508A listing mark.

2.1 ENCLOSURE
The enclosure shall be NEMA 4X stainless steel with a gasketed door and a three-point latch.
A data pocket on the inside of the door shall hold the as-built drawings.

2.2 CONTROL POWER
Control power shall be 120 VAC derived from a control power transformer with primary and secondary fusing.

2.3 PUMP CONTROLS
Each pump shall have a Hand-Off-Auto selector switch, a green run light and a red fail light.
The panel shall alternate the lead pump after each pumping cycle.
Pumps shall stop at the off float only. Stopping at an intermediate level is not acceptable.

2.4 ALARMS
The panel shall provide a red alarm beacon visible from 100 feet that is activated by high wet well level.
Seal failure and motor over-temperature for each pump shall be annunciated on the panel and communicated to the owner's SCADA system.

3.1 TESTING
The manufacturer shall perform a witnessed functional test of every control sequence before shipment.
